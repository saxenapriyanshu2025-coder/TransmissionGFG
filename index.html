FILE_026.TXTCLEARANCE: ALL STUDENTS

Six realms have gone dark. Every one of them needs a fix that does not exist yet.

For one weekend, the GeeksForGeeks Student Chapter at Bennett University turns the campus into HQ. Pick a realm. Build a squad of 2 to 4. Then you have 24 hours to ship something that brings it back.

Mentors from the industry will guide you. We cover the food, the Wi-Fi and the chai. You just have to show up and build.
